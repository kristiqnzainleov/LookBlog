Cookie Policy

LookBlog uses only a few small cookies and browser storage, all needed for the site to work. No advertising or tracking cookies.

  1. Cookies we use
  2. Browser storage
  3. Third parties
  4. Your choices

Cookies we use

  • lb_session — keeps you logged in. Strictly necessary. Lasts up to 30 days (or until you close the browser, if you didn’t choose “remember me”).
  • lb_accounts — remembers the other accounts you are logged in to on this browser, so you can switch quickly. Strictly necessary for that feature. Lasts up to 30 days.

Browser storage

Some preferences are saved only in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers: your site language, feed tab, video quality, subtitles and theater mode choices, recent searches, sound settings, the reply sort order and your cookie choice. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

Third parties

  • Fonts are served by LookBlog itself — nothing is loaded from Google Fonts.
  • YouTube: nothing from YouTube loads until you play a YouTube song or video (or a story you watch plays one, if you allowed content from other sites). It then plays in YouTube’s visible player from youtube-nocookie.com, which doesn’t set YouTube’s tracking cookies until you interact with the player itself; YouTube’s own rules apply inside it.
  • Links to other sites (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Spotify, SoundCloud, Vimeo) show a “Load” button first: nothing loads from that site until you tap it. After that, its own cookie rules apply inside its player.
  • GIFs, maps and translation: GIF searches go to GIPHY through our server (GIPHY doesn’t see who searched), but the GIF pictures themselves load from GIPHY, which sees your IP address. Event maps load from OpenStreetMap. Both load by themselves only if you allowed content from other sites; and translating sends that text to a translation service. None of these set cookies on LookBlog.
  • We don’t use analytics or advertising cookies, and no other company tracks you on LookBlog.

Your choices

The first time you visit, a cookie window asks what you allow:

  • Necessary (lb_session, lb_accounts) — always on, because logging in can’t work without them. You can delete them by logging out or clearing your browser’s cookies for this site.
  • Content from other sites — when you allow it (“Accept all”), YouTube songs in stories start by themselves and GIFs (GIPHY) and event maps (OpenStreetMap) show right away. With “Only necessary”, each of them waits until you tap it.

Your choice is saved in your browser (local storage, lb_consent), not on our servers. You can change it any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page or in Settings. Questions? Contact us.
