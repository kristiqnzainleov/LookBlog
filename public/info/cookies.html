Cookie Policy

LookBlog uses only a few small cookies and browser storage, all needed for the site to work. No advertising or tracking cookies.

  1. Cookies we use
  2. Browser storage
  3. Third parties
  4. Your choices

Cookies we use

  • lb_session — keeps you logged in. Strictly necessary. Lasts up to 30 days (or until you close the browser, if you didn’t choose “remember me”).
  • lb_accounts — remembers the other accounts you are logged in to on this browser, so you can switch quickly. Strictly necessary for that feature. Lasts up to 30 days.

Browser storage

Some preferences are saved only in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers: your site language, feed tab, video quality, subtitles and theater mode choices, recent searches, and the reply sort order. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google’s sign-in window is loaded and Google’s own cookie rules apply there. If you translate a message, its text is sent to a translation service. We don’t use analytics or advertising cookies from other companies.

Your choices

Because these cookies are needed for logging in, the site can’t work without them. You can delete them at any time by logging out or clearing your browser’s cookies for this site.
