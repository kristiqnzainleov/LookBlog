Cookie Policy

LookBlog uses only a few small cookies and browser storage, all needed for the site to work. No advertising or tracking cookies.

  1. Cookies we use
  2. Browser storage
  3. Third parties
  4. Your choices

Cookies we use

  • lb_session — keeps you logged in. Strictly necessary. Lasts up to 30 days (or until you close the browser, if you didn’t choose “remember me”).
  • lb_accounts — remembers the other accounts you are logged in to on this browser, so you can switch quickly. Strictly necessary for that feature. Lasts up to 30 days.

Browser storage

Some preferences are saved only in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers: your site language, feed tab, video quality, subtitles and theater mode choices, recent searches, sound settings and the reply sort order. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

Third parties

  • Fonts are served by LookBlog itself — nothing is loaded from Google Fonts.
  • YouTube: nothing from YouTube loads until you (or a story you watch) play a YouTube song or video. It then plays in YouTube’s visible player from youtube-nocookie.com, which doesn’t set YouTube’s tracking cookies until you interact with the player itself; YouTube’s own rules apply inside it.
  • Links to other sites (TikTok, Instagram, Spotify, SoundCloud, Vimeo…) show their player only when you open them; their own cookie rules apply there.
  • GIFs, maps and translation: searching for a GIF sends your search to GIPHY, event maps load from OpenStreetMap, and translating sends that text to a translation service. None of these set cookies on LookBlog.
  • We don’t use analytics or advertising cookies, and no other company tracks you on LookBlog.

Your choices

Because these cookies are needed for logging in, the site can’t work without them, so we don’t ask for consent for them. You can delete them at any time by logging out or clearing your browser’s cookies for this site. Questions? Contact us.
