Help Center

Quick answers to the most common questions. Logged in? The full help, with a way to message our team, is in the menu with your name → Help.

Your account

  • Sign up with your email or with Google from the home page.
  • Forgot your password? On the log in screen choose “Forgot it?”, type the email of your account and pick a new password.
  • Change your photo, banner, name, @username or bio on your profile — click the part you want to change.
  • Several accounts? Menu → Switch account. You can stay logged in to up to five.

Posting

  • Use + Create at the top to write a post, upload a short or a video, create an event — and, with the right role, a movie, a series or a song.
  • Tag people by typing @ and their name.
  • Videos and shorts can be Public, Unlisted (link only) or Private.

Messages, calls and groups

  • You can message people who follow you back. Groups are open to everyone who joins.
  • Start a voice or video call from the top of a chat. During a call you can share your screen or play a game.
  • In groups, voice channels work like a room: join, talk, share your screen and play sounds from the soundboard.

Privacy and safety

  • Make your account private: Menu → Privacy & blocking.
  • Block or report someone from the ⋯ button on their profile. They won’t be told.
  • See and clear what you watched in Menu → History.

Still stuck?

Log in and open Menu → Help → Still need help? to message the LookBlog team.
