LookBlog is a place to share what you see — photos, posts, shorts, videos, music and films — with people, not ads.

What LookBlog is

LookBlog is a social network for people who make things and people who like looking at them. You can post photos and thoughts, upload shorts and longer videos, release songs, publish films and series, chat, call, and hang out in groups with text and voice channels.

What we believe

  • People first. Your feed is built from the people you follow and the things you actually engage with. You can always switch to a simple newest-first feed.
  • Your work is yours. You keep the rights to everything you post. We only use it to show it on LookBlog.
  • Respect. Block, report and private accounts are always one click away.
  • Creators deserve good tools. Analytics, playlists, series, a music player and theater mode are free for everyone who makes things.

What you can do

  • Post photos, text and polls; react with Cool, likes and replies.
  • Upload shorts and videos with subtitles, quality options and theater mode.
  • Filmmakers, producers, photographers and creators can publish movies and Netflix-style series.
  • Singers, rappers and DJs / producers can release songs with a Spotify-style player.
  • Message friends, make voice and video calls, share your screen and play chess together.
  • Join groups with channels, roles, voice rooms, events and a soundboard.
  • Host public events that anyone can join.

Contact

Questions or problems? Open the menu with your name and choose Help → Still need help? to write to the LookBlog team. We read every message.
