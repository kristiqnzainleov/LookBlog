Privacy Policy

What information LookBlog collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, @username, email and password (stored only as a secure hash). If you use Google, we receive your name, email and Google account ID.
  • What you post: posts, photos, videos, songs, comments, messages, stories, notes, events and group content.
  • Activity: follows, likes, Cools, views, watch time, plays, searches you make on the site, and when you are online.
  • Technical data: your browser and device type and IP address, used for security and to stop abuse.

2. How we use it

  • To run LookBlog: show your content, deliver messages and calls, and keep you logged in.
  • To recommend posts and videos (“For you”) based on what you follow, watch and like.
  • To give creators analytics about their own content. Analytics show numbers and, for your biggest fans, public profile names — never your private messages.
  • To keep the service safe: fight spam, abuse and illegal content.

3. Who can see what

  • Public posts and your profile can be seen by other LookBlog users. Private accounts show posts only to approved followers.
  • Direct messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Group messages are visible to the group (or anyone, for public groups).
  • Calls go directly between browsers; LookBlog only passes on the set-up messages and doesn’t record calls.

4. Sharing with others

We don’t sell your personal information and we don’t show ads. We may share information when the law requires it, to protect people from harm, or with service providers who help us run LookBlog (for example, translation of a message you choose to translate).

5. Your choices

  • Edit or delete your posts at any time.
  • Make your account private, block people, and turn off post notifications.
  • Pause or clear your watch history in Menu → History.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or for your account to be deleted through Help.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. Stories disappear after 24 hours. When you delete something, it is removed from the site straight away and from our storage soon after, unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Children

LookBlog is not for children under 13. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 13, we close it.

8. Changes and contact

If we change this policy in an important way, we’ll tell you on the site. Questions? Write to us through Help → Still need help?
