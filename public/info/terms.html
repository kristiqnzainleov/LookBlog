If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian. You are responsible for keeping your password and recovery codes safe and for everything that happens on your account.

2. Your content

You own what you post. By posting it you give LookBlog a free, worldwide, non-exclusive licence to store, show, copy (for example, to make smaller video sizes) and share it inside LookBlog so the service can work. This licence ends when you delete the content, except where others have already shared it or the law requires us to keep it.

Only post things you have the right to post. Music, films and videos must be yours or used with permission. Mark photos and videos made with AI tools as “Made with AI” (LookBlog also labels them automatically when the file says so).

3. What’s not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate against people or groups.
  • Sexual content involving minors — ever. We report it to the authorities.
  • Nudity or sexual content in public places of the site.
  • Violence, self-harm encouragement, or dangerous challenges.
  • Sensitive content: graphic violence, blood and gore, weapons, self-harm or suicide, dead bodies and other disturbing things.
  • Spam, scams, fake accounts or pretending to be someone else.
  • Anything illegal, and breaking other people’s rights, including copyright.
  • Trying to break, overload or get around the security of the service.

4. How we moderate content

  • Automatic checks: when photos, videos and text are posted, LookBlog checks them automatically (in your browser and on our server) for nudity, graphic or disturbing content and AI-generated media. Nudity is blurred and labelled; disturbing content is refused; AI media gets a label.
  • Reports: anyone can report a post, a live or a person (and one of their messages) from its ⋯ menu — including “Illegal content” and “Uses my work without permission”. For anything else (a comment, a story, a group) send us the link through the contact form, topic “Illegal or harmful content”, and say why. Reports are confidential, and you don’t need an account to send one.
  • People decide: the LookBlog team reviews reports. One automatic step exists: a post reported as disturbing by 3 different people is hidden straight away, and a person can look at it again.
  • What we can do: leave it, label or blur it, remove it, or limit, suspend or close an account. Repeated or serious breaking of these rules can close an account.

5. When we act on your content

If we remove your content or limit your account, we tell you with a notification: what we did, which rule it broke, whether it was automatic or decided by a person, and how to disagree. If you think we got it wrong, write to us at /contact or Menu → Help → Still need help? within six months, and a person who wasn’t involved will look again and answer you. You can also take a dispute to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body or to a court.

6. Copyright

If something on LookBlog uses your work without permission, report it with “Uses my work without permission”, or send us a notice through /contact (topic: Copyright) with: your name and contact, the work, the link to what copies it, and a statement that you are the owner or act for them. We remove infringing content quickly and close the accounts of repeat infringers.
